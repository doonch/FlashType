❓ Help & Guide

🚀 Quick Start

Start Here

1. Pick language and lesson: Click any country flag in the toolbar at the top (or click the active lesson banner) and choose a lesson to practice.

2. Start typing in answers: Type your answer in the text box and press Enter (or click Submit) to test yourself and build muscle memory.

3. When you're ready, press the play button (▶): Switch to hands-free Passive Mode anytime to listen to questions and answers spoken out loud on an automated audio loop!

🧠 Philosophy

The thinking behind FlashType is that by forcing you to type in the correct answer, we create new connections in your brain and build up muscle memory.

With typical flashcards, passive recognition often tricks you into believing you know a word when you just recognize it. By requiring each keystroke of the spelling, tone, or romanization, FlashType stimulates active motor recall. Your fingers learn the patterns, solidifying language skills in long-term memory.

📖 How to use this app?

🏳️ Flags
Switch between languages and open the full list of lessons for that language.
⌨️ Typing Stage
Type your answer and press Enter (or click Submit). Correct entries advance you immediately; incorrect answers provide instant guidance until mastered.
🔊 Audio & Speech
Questions and answers are read aloud with speech synthesis so you develop authentic pronunciation and listening comprehension.
📜 Show List
Expand or collapse the full vocabulary list of the current lesson to preview characters, romanizations, and meanings.
⚙️ Settings
Select Cantonese romanization (Jyutping vs. Yale), toggle speech reading for questions and answers, and customize Passive Mode timers.
✨ AI Sentences
Synthesize original practice sentences using Gemini AI, with options to select your language and completed lesson vocabulary pool.
📥 / 📤 Download & Upload
Save active lesson pairs or paste and load your own custom vocabulary files in simple question:answer format.

🎧 Passive Mode

What it does: Passive Mode runs a continuous, automated loop through questions and answers with speech playback—requiring zero typing. It's designed for hands-free immersion while walking, driving, exercising, or relaxing.

💡 Pro Tip: When a question is shown and read, try to say the correct answer out loud before the countdown timer expires and the answer is revealed on screen!

Setting timers to your comfort level: Open Settings (⚙) to configure two timing parameters:

  • Question delay: The duration (seconds) the question stays on screen before revealing the answer (default: 3.5s). Increase this if you want more time to formulate your answer.
  • Answer decay delay: How long the revealed answer remains on screen before automatically cycling to the next question (default: 2.5s).
